## Hi, I'm Giovanni 👋

My group at the University of Warwick develops machine learning methods for
**reinforcement learning**, **autonomous decision-making** and **AI for medical imaging**,
from theory to real-world deployment.

### Selected open-source work

- **[modMARL](https://github.com/gmontana/modMARL)**: cooperative multi-agent RL with
  agent-to-agent communication. 31 algorithms, each with tests and recorded learning evidence.
- **[X-Raydar](https://github.com/gmontana/xraydar-cv)**: reads chest X-rays for 37 findings,
  trained on 2.5 million NHS studies, with a companion [report-labelling model](https://github.com/gmontana/xraydar-nlp).
  [*The Lancet Digital Health*](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landig/article/PIIS2589-7500(23)00218-2/fulltext)
- **[BREST](https://github.com/gmontana/BREST)**: 3-year breast cancer risk prediction from
  screening mammograms, developed with NHS Breast Screening Programme data.
- **[Decoding Viewer Emotions](https://github.com/gmontana/DecodingViewerEmotions)**: predicts
  viewers' emotional responses to video ads.
  [*Scientific Reports*, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-76968-9)
- **[Collaborative Pick and Place](https://github.com/gmontana/CollaborativePickAndPlaceEnv)**:
  a grid-world where agents with complementary roles must coordinate hand-overs.
- **[Stanza](https://github.com/gmontana/stanza)** and **[C2Zig](https://github.com/gmontana/C2Zig)**:
  a dependency-free line editor for Zig, and a Zig tutorial for C programmers.
